Questions

Do I pay online?

No. Your payment choice (PayPal, Cash App, Zelle or Chime) is only a preference. The owner confirms payment details with you after approval.

What do I need to rent?

A valid driver's license. The owner explains how to verify your ID after your request is approved. Never send ID photos by email or through the request form.

How do I know a car is free for my dates?

Search with your dates and only available cars appear. The owner still confirms every request.

Can I cancel?

Contact the owner as soon as your plans change. See each car page for the cancellation policy.
